Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information asaplimo (gbubemi.uk) collects, why, who we share it with and what choices you have. The Service is a personal project created and run by Mitchell Akambe ("we", "us") from Nigeria, who is the controller of your data.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, name and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile details. The display name and profile photo you set in Settings. A photo you upload is resized in your browser and stored as a small image with your account.
  • Chats. Your messages, the AI replies, the AI's visible reasoning (for models that provide it), conversation titles and timestamps. Pasted text and uploaded text or code files are sent as part of your messages.
  • Images. An image you attach is sent to the AI model to answer your message. In your saved history it appears only as "[image attached]"; the image itself is not stored in your chat history.
  • Account and plan details. Your usage count, any usage limit and when it ends, how many times your usage has been limited, whether you have Premium or an unlock code, your selected AI model and your Stripe customer ID.
  • Payment information. Card details you type in the payment form go directly to Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider and the services we use may automatically process your IP address, browser type and request logs to deliver the Service and keep it secure.
  • Settings saved in your browser. Your login token and your light or dark theme choice. See the Cookie Policy.

2. How we use information

  • to sign you in and keep you signed in;
  • to answer your messages by sending them to AI models;
  • to save your chats and generate short chat titles;
  • to run your free trial, subscription and unlock codes;
  • to keep the Service secure and prevent abuse;
  • to respond to you and meet legal duties.

Our legal bases, where the law requires one, are performing our contract with you (running the Service), our legitimate interests in security and improving a small service, your consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show ads. We do not use your chats to train AI models ourselves.

3. Who we share information with

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf or under their own terms:

  • Google for sign-in (Google OAuth) and for the Gemini AI models and chat title generation. Your messages and any attached images are sent to Google when you use Gemini models.
  • Groq and OpenAI. When you use the GPT-OSS model, your messages are sent to Groq, which runs the model.
  • Stripe for payments and subscriptions.
  • Cloudflare for hosting the Service and storing account and chat data.

These providers have their own privacy policies and may handle data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

Please do not put sensitive information in your chats. Treat anything you send to an AI provider as something that leaves our control.

4. International transfers

We are in Nigeria and our providers operate in other countries, including the United States. By using the Service you understand your data is transferred to and processed in those countries.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account and chats until you ask us to delete them or we close the Service. Login sessions expire after 30 days. Payment records may be kept by Stripe as the law requires. Backups and provider logs may keep data for a short time after deletion.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Nigeria's data protection law, the UK and EU GDPR and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict some processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us below. You can change your display name and photo in Settings and log out at any time.

7. Children

The Service is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. Users aged 13 to 17 should use the Service with a parent or guardian's permission.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures such as HTTPS and access controls. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that your data will never be accessed without permission.

9. Third-party links and tools

The Service may link to or load third-party services such as Google and Stripe. Their handling of your data is covered by their own policies.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will make it clear on the Service.
