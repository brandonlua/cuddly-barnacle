About asaplimo

asaplimo is a personal AI chat project created by Mitchell Akambe from Nigeria.

He builds Minecraft mods and web apps for fun, and this is the biggest thing he has made so far.

The chat runs on third-party AI models: Gemini Flash Lite and GPT-OSS 120B are free, and Gemini 3.1 Pro is available on Premium. You can switch between them from the model picker in the chat.
