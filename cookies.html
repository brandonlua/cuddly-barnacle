Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what cookies and similar browser storage asaplimo (gbubemi.uk) uses. It is part of our Privacy Policy.

1. What these are

Cookies are small files a website places on your device. Local storage is a similar feature built into your browser that lets a site save small pieces of data. Both help a site remember you between visits.

2. What we store

asaplimo itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It saves the following items in your browser's local storage, all needed for the Service to work or to remember your choices:

NamePurposeDuration
asaplimo_session_tokenKeeps you logged in so you do not have to sign in on every page.Until you log out or clear your browser data. The login itself expires after 30 days.
asaplimo_themeRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode.Until you clear your browser data.
asaplimo_thinkRemembers whether you turned on the Think button.Until you clear your browser data.
asaplimo_consentRemembers that you accepted the Terms of Service, Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy, and which version, so we do not ask again.Until you clear your browser data or we publish updated terms.

3. Third-party cookies and storage

  • Google. When you sign in, Google may set its own cookies on its sign-in pages to keep your account secure and remember your sign-in. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Stripe. When you open the payment form under Settings › Plan, we load Stripe's payment script. Stripe may set cookies or use browser storage to process payments securely and detect fraud. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Fonts and icons. We load fonts from Google Fonts and some icons from public content delivery networks, which may receive your IP address when they are requested.

4. Other data we handle

Information we store on our servers, such as your account details and chats, is described in the Privacy Policy.

5. We do not use

  • advertising cookies;
  • analytics or tracking cookies;
  • cross-site tracking or profiling.

6. Your choices

Because we only use storage that is strictly necessary or that remembers your own settings, we do not show a cookie banner for optional cookies. You are asked once to accept our Terms of Service, Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy before using the Service. You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings, or by using Log out in Settings, which removes your login token. If you block this storage, you will not be able to stay signed in. You can also block third-party cookies in your browser, but the Google sign-in or the payment form may stop working.

7. Changes

If we start using other cookies or tracking, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
